    What is CanSat?

    CanSat is an educational program of the European Space Agency (ESA).

    1. 01

      Build

      Fit everything inside a can-sized cylinder.

    2. 02

      Launch

      The CanSat is taken up high and released.

    3. 03

      Measure

      While it descends it measures temperature and pressure.

    4. 04

      Compete

      The best teams go on to the European competition.

    Learn more on the ESA CanSat page

    The Kaiper Index: mapping an invisible ocean

    Just as the Kuiper Belt hides oceans beneath its icy surface, the atmosphere hides its own invisible ocean.

    Could liquid water exist here?

    Habitability index

    Humidity, temperature, and UV radiation.

    Standard, well-tested sensors

    Is this environment stable?

    Atmospheric turbulence map

    An inertial sensor (IMU) tracks the stability of the descent.

    Our safest bet

    Would it be shielded from radiation?

    Cosmic radiation shielding gradient

    We measure how background radiation changes with altitude.

    The honest question mark

    Each of the three sensors on its own is a fairly standard, well-tested piece of kit.

    The turbulence data from the IMU is the part we’re most confident will simply work.

    The radiation sensor is the one honest question mark.
